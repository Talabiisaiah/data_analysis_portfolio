# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard – Excel Dashboard Project

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes and visualizes **sales performance** across multiple store locations of a coffee shop chain from **January to June**. The report is aimed at providing clear business insights into sales patterns, product trends, and customer behavior.


## 🧾 Project Summary

**Client**: Coffee Shop Chain (fictional)  
**Objective**: To track key sales performance indicators and customer transaction patterns using a visual and interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel**
- **Pivot Tables & Charts**
- **Slicers & Timeline Filters**
- **Power Query (Data Cleaning)**
- **Custom Formulas and Dynamic Ranges**

---
## 📊 Key Metrics Tracked

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Total Transactions** | Total number of purchases made (214,470) |
| **Monthly Revenue** | Revenue trends from Jan to Jun |
| **Transactions by Weekday** | Customer activity pattern across the week |
| **Transactions by Time Range** | Peak business hours |
| **Revenue by Store Location** | Comparison of revenue across Astoria, Hell’s Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan |
| **Top Products & Categories** | Best-selling items based on transaction volume and revenue |

---

## 🧠 Business Insights

### 💡 Revenue Trends
- 📈 Revenue peaked in **June** ($166,486), followed by **May** ($156,728), indicating increasing customer activity toward mid-year.
- 📉 January and February showed the lowest performance, possibly due to post-holiday slumps.

### 🕑 Peak Hours
- ⏰ Most transactions occurred between **6–8 AM**, reflecting strong morning coffee demand.

### 🗓️ Weekly Activity
- **Fridays** saw the highest transaction count (21,701), while **Saturdays** lagged (20,510), suggesting a weekday-heavy business model.

### 🏪 Store Performance
- All three locations performed similarly, with **Hell’s Kitchen** slightly ahead in revenue ($236,511).

### 🔝 Top Product Categories
- **Coffee** and **Tea** dominated sales, with **Bakery items** a distant third.
